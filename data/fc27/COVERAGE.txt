KNOWN LIMITATIONS OF WHAT IS SHIPPED
------------------------------------------------------------
See NOTICE.txt for provenance and licensing. In summary:
  - ratings.json is a LOSSY PES-25 projection; use ea_attrs.json (34 raw).
  - formation slots are side-specific, player positions are coarse; bridge
    them with ea_positions.json slotCompat (7887/7887 starters fit).
  - ovr_weights.json / playstyles.json attribute ids use an UNRESOLVED EA
    enum. Shipped raw with warnings; do not index ea_attrs.json with them.
  - traits.json bitmasks are raw: no defensible PlayStyle mapping was found.
  - style/form/inj in ratings.json are placeholders (single-valued).
  - affinities.json emotion scale is inferred, not documented.
  - 452/717 managers are named; the rest are upstream placeholders.
  - Libertadores (1003) / Sudamericana (1014) seeds are outside the 717-club
    universe; their names ship in external_teams.json.
